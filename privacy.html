Privacy policy

This policy covers ai-context-sync, Frank Herb's personal tool for copying one notes folder between his Chromebook and his own Google Drive.

What it accesses

When Frank signs in, the tool can read and write files in his Google Drive. He uses that access to keep one folder in sync. The sign-in secret stays on his computer.

What it does not do

The tool is not offered to other people. It does not sell information, show ads, or send the notes to anyone else.
